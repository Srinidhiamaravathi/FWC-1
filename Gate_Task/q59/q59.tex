\noindent
\textbf{Question 59}

Two products are sold from a vending machine, which has two push buttons
$P_1$ and $P_2$. When a button is pressed, the price of the corresponding
product is displayed in a 7-segment display.

\begin{itemize}
\item If no buttons are pressed, `0' is displayed, signifying Rs.~0.
\item If only $P_1$ is pressed, `2' is displayed, signifying Rs.~2.
\item If only $P_2$ is pressed, `5' is displayed, signifying Rs.~5.
\item If both $P_1$ and $P_2$ are pressed, `E' is displayed, signifying `Error'.
\end{itemize}

Consider:

\begin{itemize}
\item[(a)] push button pressed/not pressed $\rightarrow$ logic 1/0.
\item[(b)] segment glowing/not glowing $\rightarrow$ logic 1/0.
\end{itemize}

If a segment output is considered as a function of $P_1$ and $P_2$,
which of the following is correct?

\begin{enumerate}
\item $g = \overline{P_1} + P_2, \quad d = c + e$
\item $g = P_1 + P_2, \quad d = c + e$
\item $g = \overline{P_1} + P_2, \quad e = b + c$
\item $g = P_1 + P_2, \quad e = b + c$
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.7cm}

%------------------------------------------------
% Solution
%------------------------------------------------
\noindent
\textbf{Solution}

\vspace{0.3cm}

From the display requirements:

\[
\begin{array}{c c c}
P_1 & P_2 & \text{Output} \\
0 & 0 & 0 \\
1 & 0 & 2 \\
0 & 1 & 5 \\
1 & 1 & E
\end{array}
\]

\vspace{0.4cm}

From the standard 7-segment patterns:

\[
\begin{array}{c c c c c c c c}
\text{Character} & a & b & c & d & e & f & g \\
0 & 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 & 0 \\
2 & 1 & 1 & 0 & 1 & 1 & 0 & 1 \\
5 & 1 & 0 & 1 & 1 & 0 & 1 & 1 \\
E & 1 & 0 & 0 & 1 & 1 & 1 & 1
\end{array}
\]

\vspace{0.4cm}

Observing segment \textbf{g}:

It is OFF only when $P_1=0$ and $P_2=0$.  
Hence,

\[
g = P_1 + P_2
\]

\vspace{0.4cm}

Observing segment \textbf{e}:

It is ON in cases corresponding to segments $b$ or $c$.  
Hence,

\[
e = b + c
\]

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{center}
\textbf{Correct Option: 4}
\end{center}
