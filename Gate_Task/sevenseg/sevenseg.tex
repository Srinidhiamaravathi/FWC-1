% Question
\noindent
\bluehead{Question 59}

Two products are sold from a vending machine with push buttons $P_1$ and $P_2$.

\begin{itemize}
\item No button → display 0  
\item Only $P_1$ → display 2  
\item Only $P_2$ → display 5  
\item Both pressed → display E  
\end{itemize}

Pressed = 1, Not pressed = 0.  
Segment ON = 1.

\vspace{0.6cm}

% Truth Table
\bluehead{Truth Table}

\[
\begin{array}{c c c}
P_1 & P_2 & \text{Output} \\
0 & 0 & 0 \\
1 & 0 & 2 \\
0 & 1 & 5 \\
1 & 1 & E
\end{array}
\]

\vspace{0.6cm}

% Segment Table
\bluehead{7-Segment Pattern}

\[
\begin{array}{c c c c c c c c}
\text{Char} & a & b & c & d & e & f & g \\
0 & 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 & 0 \\
2 & 1 & 1 & 0 & 1 & 1 & 0 & 1 \\
5 & 1 & 0 & 1 & 1 & 0 & 1 & 1 \\
E & 1 & 0 & 0 & 1 & 1 & 1 & 1
\end{array}
\]

\vspace{0.6cm}

% Logic
\bluehead{Logic Expressions}

\[
\begin{aligned}
a &= 1 \\
b &= \overline{P_2} \\
c &= \overline{P_1} \\
d &= 1 \\
e &= P_1 + \overline{P_2} \\
f &= \overline{P_1} + P_2 \\
g &= P_1 + P_2
\end{aligned}
\]

\vspace{0.6cm}

% Conclusion
\bluehead{Conclusion}

The required segment outputs are derived from the button combinations.
These expressions can be implemented using logic gates or programmed
into a controller for hardware realization.
