\section*{Question}

Identify the Boolean expression implemented by the given circuit.

\section*{Analysis}

From circuit analysis the output is

$Q = A' + B$

Truth table:

\begin{tabular}{c c c}
$A$ & $B$ & $Q$ \\
0 & 0 & 1 \\
0 & 1 & 1 \\
1 & 0 & 0 \\
1 & 1 & 1 \\
\end{tabular}

\section*{Python Code}

\begin{lstlisting}
import numpy as np
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

A = np.array([0,0,1,1])
B = np.array([0,1,0,1])

Q = (~A & 1) | B

print("A B Q")

for i in range(4):
    print(A[i], B[i], Q[i])

x = np.arange(4)

plt.step(x,A,where='post',label='A')
plt.step(x,B,where='post',label='B')
plt.step(x,Q,where='post',label='Q')

plt.grid()
plt.legend()

plt.savefig("graph.png")

plt.show()
\end{lstlisting}
