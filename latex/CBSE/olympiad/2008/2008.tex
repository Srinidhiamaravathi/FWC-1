Srinidhi A\\
ID: COMETFWC053

49th International Mathematical Olympiad\\
Madrid (Spain), July 2008
\end{center}

\begin{enumerate}

\item
An acute angled triangle $ABC$ has orthocentre $H$.
The circle passing through $H$ with centre the midpoint of $BC$
intersects $BC$ at points $A_1$ and $A_2$.
Similarly define points $B_1, B_2, C_1, C_2$.
Show that the six points
$A_1, A_2, B_1, B_2, C_1, C_2$
lie on a circle.

\item
\begin{enumerate}
\item Prove that  
$x^2/(x-1)^2 + y^2/(y-1)^2 + z^2/(z-1)^2 >= 1$  
for all real numbers $x, y, z$ not equal to $1$
satisfying $x y z = 1$.

\item Prove that equality holds for infinitely many
rational triples $(x, y, z)$.
\end{enumerate}

\item
Prove that there exist infinitely many positive integers $n$
such that $n^2 + 1$ has a prime divisor greater than
$2n + (2n)^{1/2}$.

\item
Find all functions $f$ from $(0, +)$ to $(0, +)$ such that  
$(f(w)^2 + f(x)^2)/(f(y^2) + f(z^2)) =
(w^2 + x^2)/(y^2 + z^2)$  
for all positive real numbers $w, x, y, z$
satisfying $w x = y z$.

\item
Let $n$ and $k$ be positive integers with $k >= n$
and $k - n$ even.
There are $2n$ lamps, initially all off.
At each step one lamp is switched.

Let $N$ be the number of sequences of $k$ steps
resulting in lamps $1$ to $n$ on
and lamps $n+1$ to $2n$ off.

Let $M$ be the number of such sequences
where lamps $n+1$ to $2n$ are never switched on.
Find $N/M$.

\item
Let $ABCD$ be a convex quadrilateral with
$|BA|$ not equal to $|BC|$.
Let the incircles of triangles $ABC$ and $ADC$
be considered.
Suppose a circle is tangent to ray $BA$ beyond $A$
and ray $BC$ beyond $C$,
and also tangent to lines $AD$ and $CD$.
Prove that the common external tangents
of the two incircles intersect on this circle.

\end{enumerate}
