Srinidhi A\\  
ID: COMETFWCO53  

49th International Mathematical Olympiad  
Madrid (Spain), July 2008
\end{center}

\begin{enumerate}

\item
An acute-angled triangle $ABC$ has orthocentre $H$.
The circle passing through $H$ with centre the midpoint of $BC$
intersects $BC$ at $A_1$ and $A_2$.
Similarly define points $B_1, B_2, C_1, C_2$.
Show that $A_1, A_2, B_1, B_2, C_1, C_2$ lie on a circle.

\item
\begin{enumerate}
\item Prove that
\[
\frac{x^2}{(x-1)^2} + \frac{y^2}{(y-1)^2} + \frac{z^2}{(z-1)^2} \ge 1
\]
for all real numbers $x,y,z \ne 1$ satisfying $xyz = 1$.

\item Prove that equality holds for infinitely many rational triples
$(x,y,z)$.
\end{enumerate}

\item
Prove that there exist infinitely many positive integers $n$ such that
$n^2 + 1$ has a prime divisor greater than $2n + \sqrt{2n}$.

\item
Find all functions $f : (0,\infty) \to (0,\infty)$ such that
\[
\frac{f(w)^2 + f(x)^2}{f(y^2) + f(z^2)} =
\frac{w^2 + x^2}{y^2 + z^2}
\]
for all positive real numbers $w,x,y,z$ satisfying $wx = yz$.

\item
Let $n$ and $k$ be positive integers with $k \ge n$ and $k-n$ even.
There are $2n$ lamps, initially all off.
At each step one lamp is switched.

Let $N$ be the number of sequences of $k$ steps resulting in lamps
$1$ through $n$ on and lamps $n+1$ through $2n$ off.

Let $M$ be the number of such sequences where lamps $n+1$ through $2n$
are never switched on.
Determine $N/M$.

\item
Let $ABCD$ be a convex quadrilateral with $|BA| \ne |BC|$.
Let $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2$ be the incircles of triangles $ABC$ and $ADC$.
Suppose a circle $\omega$ is tangent to ray $BA$ beyond $A$ and ray $BC$
beyond $C$, and also tangent to lines $AD$ and $CD$.
Prove that the common external tangents of $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2$
intersect on $\omega$.

\end{enumerate}
